# GOV.UK Design System

## When to use

Use for transactional journeys: asking for information, helping users check answers, confirming completion, and recovering from validation errors. The pattern guidance connects a task to component choices and written explanations.

## What to extract

Study the question sequence and the route back from an error. Make the requested information, reason for asking, expected format, and next action clear. Compare a single-page form with a staged flow using the actual user's task, not a blanket preference for either.

## What not to copy

UK addresses, identifiers, policy assumptions, and government branding are contextual. Adapt the pattern to the product's locale and audience. A borrowed form pattern still needs testing with realistic data and error states.
