# W3C Internationalization

## When to use

Use when language, script, writing direction, or locale changes how content is entered, displayed, or understood. Internationalization is part of the interface structure, not a translation pass after layout is finished.

## What to extract

Consult the relevant language and direction guidance. Test real translated content, mixed scripts, text expansion, and locale-sensitive inputs rather than relying on a single mockup. Identify where the design assumes a particular name structure, date order, writing direction, or word length.

## What not to copy

Do not mirror every icon mechanically for RTL, assume all languages use Latin spacing rules, or enforce one country's data format globally. Follow the applicable source and test with people and content from the target locale; a historical typography principle is not locale expertise.
